# Personal Wiki

A private library that turns retained external material into attributable, connected reading notes. The same material can be captured more than once without becoming a new source.

## Language

**Source**:
A distinct external material that the library identifies across captures, such as an author thread, a deliberately selected reply, or an article.
_Avoid_: Capture, note

**Submitted anchor**:
The specific post the owner originally selected, even when it belongs to a larger author thread.
_Avoid_: Conversation root

**Focus**:
The material the owner intends to save and understand; surrounding context supports it without replacing it.
_Avoid_: Entire conversation

**Author thread**:
An identified sequence of an author's posts that continues one material. An author's response to another participant is not automatically a continuation of that thread.
_Avoid_: Every post by the author, entire conversation

**Context**:
Material needed to interpret the focus, including parent posts and the conversation root.
_Avoid_: Main source

**Discussion**:
Other participants' responses, author clarifications, and exchanges around the focus, distinct from its main content.
_Avoid_: Author thread

**Capture**:
An immutable, dated observation of a source and its acquired context, discussion, and coverage limitations.
_Avoid_: Source, latest truth

**Asset**:
Retained source bytes, such as a raw provider response, article document, or media file.
_Avoid_: Download link

**Representation**:
A text form derived from a capture, such as extracted original text or a translation, without becoming independent evidence.
_Avoid_: Independent source

**Note**:
An attributable reading document derived from one or more captures, with a stable identity across revisions and moves.
_Avoid_: Capture, source copy

**Citation**:
A connection between a statement in a note and the particular captured material that supports it.
_Avoid_: Unattributed source list

**Topic**:
An evolving page connecting knowledge across notes and sources.
_Avoid_: Folder

**Publication**:
An accepted batch of knowledge changes with an identifiable result and revision.
_Avoid_: Agent output, capture completion

**Proposed changeset**:
A suggested batch of knowledge changes against a particular library revision, awaiting the library's acceptance.
_Avoid_: Publication

**Library revision**:
An identifiable, complete version of the library's knowledge state.
_Avoid_: Capture date

**Publication receipt**:
The retained result identifying an applied publication and its affected knowledge, even if its effects are later reversed.
_Avoid_: Agent success message

**Compensating publication**:
A new publication reversing a previous batch's active effects while preserving the record and evidence of both operations.
_Avoid_: History deletion
